Studio Viana / Your questions answered

What does Studio Viana design?

Studio Viana creates custom mural wallpapers and 3D visualizations for homes, boutiques, and brands. The studio is based in Surat, India.

  • Wallpapers shaped around a room's scale, light, and mood.
  • 3D interiors, product imagery, and spatial concepts.
  • Design enquiries through email or WhatsApp.

How much does custom wallpaper cost?

A custom wallpaper quote depends on the wall dimensions, design, paper finish, and quantity. The website provides an estimate; confirm the final specification and price with the studio before ordering.

  • Measure your wall's width and height.
  • Choose a design and paper finish.
  • Send those details to the studio for a confirmed quote.

How do I request a wallpaper design?

Sign in, open a wallpaper design, enter your wall dimensions and paper finish, and send a design request. Approved print-ready files appear in your requests and downloads.

  • Sign in to explore the collection.
  • Review the selected design, dimensions, finish, and quantity.
  • Use your downloads panel to check the request's approval status.

Who is behind Studio Viana?

Vicky Rana is the founder of Studio Viana and a digital artist and 3D visualizer based in Surat, India. His work includes mural wallpapers and spatial art.

  • Explore Vicky Rana's Behance portfolio.
  • View the studio's Adobe Stock contributor profile.
  • Email the studio about your project.
